# 📊 Product Sales Analytics Dashboard (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project showcases an **end-to-end Product Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Excel**, designed to transform raw transactional data into **actionable business insights**.

The analysis is based on **15,000+ retail transactions across 3 years (2023–2025)** and focuses on sales performance, profitability, customer payment behavior, regional trends, and operational efficiency.

This project is created from a **Business Analyst / Data Analyst** perspective and is suitable for **entry-level / fresher roles**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to support **data-driven decision-making** for key stakeholders:

* **Sales Teams:** Identify top-performing products and regions
* **Operations Teams:** Analyze shipping costs and efficiency
* **Leadership / Management:** Track KPIs, profitability, and growth opportunities

---

## 🗂 Dataset Description

The dataset represents **retail store transaction data** at a transaction level.

**Key Columns Include:**

* Order Date & Time
* Store / Region
* Product Category & Product Name
* Quantity & Unit Price
* Payment Type
* Discount
* Shipping Cost
* Sales & Profit

📅 **Time Period:** 2023 – 2025
📈 **Data Volume:** 15,000+ records

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following data preparation steps were performed before analysis:

* Removed duplicate records
* Handled missing and inconsistent values
* Standardized product names and regional labels
* Converted date fields into proper date formats
* Ensured numeric consistency for currency and quantity fields
* Created calculated columns:

  * Total Sales
  * Net Profit
  * Average Order Value (AOV)

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1️⃣ Revenue Concentration

A small group of top products contributes a significant share of total revenue.

* **Insight:** Strong product performance with dependency risk
* **Business Impact:** Opportunity to diversify revenue streams

### 2️⃣ Regional Performance Analysis

* North and Central regions generate the highest order volumes
* South region shows untapped growth potential

**Business Impact:** Enables region-specific sales and marketing strategies

### 3️⃣ Discount vs Profitability

Retail customers receive higher discounts compared to Wholesale customers.

**Business Impact:** Discount optimization can improve profit margins without increasing sales volume

### 4️⃣ Customer Payment Behavior

Digital payment methods (Credit Card & Online) dominate transactions.

**Business Impact:** Supports investment in digital payment partnerships and offers

### 5️⃣ Shipping Cost Optimization

High-value products incur higher shipping costs, impacting net profit.

**Business Impact:** Vendor negotiation or logistics optimization can improve margins
